
#  SevaPath

### Making Indian Public Services Simpler, Clearer, and More Accessible

**SevaPath** is a multilingual citizen assistant that helps people find the right Indian government service by simply describing what they need in natural language.

Instead of navigating complex government portals and trying to understand which service or scheme applies to their situation, citizens can describe their problem in their own words. SevaPath identifies the most relevant service and provides a clear path forward — including why the service is relevant, required documents, the next step, and the official government portal.

> **Built for the “Build what moves India” public-service hackathon.**

##  The Problem

Indian government digital services can be difficult to navigate, especially for citizens who:

* Don't know the exact name of the service they need
* Are unfamiliar with government terminology
* Have difficulty navigating multiple government portals
* Are using a mobile device or slower internet connection
* Prefer communicating in an Indian language
* Don't know which documents are required

The problem often begins **before the application itself** — citizens first need to figure out where they should go.

### SevaPath solves this discovery problem.

Instead of asking:

> *“Which government service should I choose?”*

SevaPath lets citizens ask:

> *“This is my situation. What should I do?”*

SevaPath uses the OpenAI API to improve understanding of natural-language citizen requests and help connect them with the appropriate service.

###  Document Checklist

Shows the documents a citizen may need before beginning the application.

###  Clear Explanation

Instead of simply displaying a government-service name, SevaPath explains **why that service is relevant** to the citizen's situation.

###  Next-Step Guidance

Provides a simple explanation of what the citizen should do next.

###  Official Government Links

Citizens are directed to the relevant official government service rather than being asked to provide sensitive information to SevaPath.

###  Voice Input

Users can describe their request using voice input where browser support is available.

###  Citizen-Friendly Interface

Designed with a simple, responsive interface for users with different levels of digital experience.

## 🔐 Safety & Privacy

SevaPath is designed as a prototype and does **not** interact with private government systems.

The project does not require or collect:

* Real Aadhaar numbers
* PAN details
* OTPs
* Passwords
* Payment information
* Real government account credentials
* Sensitive personal information

Government interactions that would normally require sensitive information are represented using mock/synthetic behavior.

SevaPath directs citizens toward official government services rather than presenting itself as an official government product.

##  Design Principles

SevaPath was designed around five principles:

**1. Start with the citizen's problem**

Citizens shouldn't need to know government terminology before asking for help.

**2. Reduce decision-making**

The system narrows down the relevant service instead of presenting a long list of portals.

**3. Explain, don't just redirect**

Citizens should understand why a service is relevant and what they need before leaving SevaPath.

**4. Design for real users**

The experience considers multilingual users, mobile users, slower connections, and people with limited digital experience.

**5. Keep sensitive interactions safe**

Personal information, payments, OTPs, and government authentication remain outside the prototype.
